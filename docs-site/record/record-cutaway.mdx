---
title: "Record a demo of Cutaway"
description: "Make a how-to video of Cutaway itself, with Cutaway's own recorder."
---

Normally Cutaway keeps all of its own windows out of your recordings, so it never gets in the way of what you're showing. If you want to **show Cutaway itself**, for example to teach someone how to use it, turn on demo mode.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the recorder">
    On the **Projects** screen, press **New recording**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the recording options">
    Press the **gear** at the right of the bottom bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Record Cutaway too (for demos)">
    A note under **Start recording** says "Cutaway will be in this recording".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to record and start">
    Pick your **Display** or an **Area**. With demo mode on, you can also pick Cutaway's own window under **Window**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## While you record

- Cutaway **stays open** instead of hiding, so you can show it.
- You can go anywhere in the app: the projects screen, another project, the editor, export. The recording keeps going.
- Stop from the floating recording controls. Cutaway goes back to the project you started in and adds the recording there.

## What's in the video

| In the video | Not in the video |
| --- | --- |
| Cutaway's window, its menus and pop-up windows | The floating recording controls |
| Everything else on your screen | The teleprompter |
| | The countdown and your camera bubble |

So you can still read a script on the [teleprompter](/record/teleprompter) while you demo.

<Note>
  Demo mode is only for the current session. Next time you open Cutaway, recordings leave Cutaway out again, so it never shows up in a normal recording by surprise.
</Note>

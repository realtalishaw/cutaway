A **layout** is how your screen and your camera are arranged. Pick one before you record. You can always change it later in the editor.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/record.png" alt="The recorder. The five layout choices are above the bar, next to 2. The camera menus are 3 and 4" />
</Frame>

## The five layouts

| Layout | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Screen + bubble** | Your screen, with your face in a small bubble in the corner. The most popular choice. |
| **Side by side** | Your screen and your face next to each other. |
| **Camera + screen** | Your face big, with your screen in a small box. |
| **Screen only** | Just your screen. No face. |
| **Camera only** | Just your face. No screen. |

## Move your camera and change its shape

With **Screen + bubble**, your camera shows in a corner of the preview before you record.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/record-bubble.png" alt="The camera bubble on the recorder's preview, marked 1, with its shape button marked 2" />
</Frame>

- **Move it:** drag it (**1**) to another corner, out of the way of what you're showing. It starts in that corner of your video too.
- **Change its shape:** hover over it and press the shape button (**2**). It switches between rounded, circle and square.

While you record, the floating camera bubble has the same shape button. Whatever shape you end on is the shape in your video. You can still change both later in the editor's **Camera** tab.

<Note>
  Layouts that show your face need a camera. If you see "This layout needs a camera", click **No camera** in the bottom bar and pick one.
</Note>

## Use your iPhone as a camera

Your iPhone can be your camera. It usually looks better than a laptop camera.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Sign in to the same Apple Account on your iPhone and Mac. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your iPhone">
    Lock your iPhone and put it near your Mac, on a stand or mount. You can also plug it in with a cable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick it in Cutaway">
    Click the camera menu in the bottom bar. Your iPhone shows up as **iPhone**. Pick it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two cameras

You can record two camera angles at once. For example, your Mac's camera from the front, and your iPhone from the side.

1. Pick your first camera in the **camera** menu (Camera A).
2. Pick your second camera in the **B** menu (Camera B).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/record-camera-b-menu.png" alt="The B menu open, marked 1, with iPhone Camera marked 2" />
</Frame>

Both cameras now show on the preview:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/record-two-cameras.png" alt="Camera A and Camera B picked in the bar, marked 1 and 2, and both camera bubbles on the preview, marked 3 and 4" />
</Frame>

Cutaway records both. While you record, the floating bubble shows one of them. Hover over it and press **A** or **B** to switch which one you see.

<Note>
  When your iPhone is the camera, its own screen goes dark or shows that it's in use. That's normal: it's still recording.
</Note>

### Find your second camera in the editor

In the first cut, Cutaway switches between the two cameras for you. To watch Camera B, switch cameras at a moment, or cut while watching it, see [Edit with two cameras](/edit/two-cameras), or watch [Record and edit with two cameras](/tutorials/two-cameras).

## Change the layout later

You can change the layout for the whole video, or just for part of it, in the editor. See [Camera layouts](/edit/camera-layouts).

---
title: "Teleprompter"
description: "Read your script while you record. It floats over your screen and is never in the video."
---

The **teleprompter** shows your script in big text that scrolls past a reading line while you talk. It sits in its own floating window, so you can put it right under your camera, at the side of your screen, or over your camera bubble. **It is never recorded.**

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter.png" alt="The teleprompter window with a script in big text. Play is marked 1, the time left 2, the pencil 3, the layout button 4, the gear 5 and the pace 6" />
</Frame>

1. **Play**: start or pause scrolling.
2. **Time left** at this pace.
3. **Pencil**: edit your script.
4. **Layout**: move it to a quick spot.
5. **Gear**: settings.
6. **Pace**, in words per minute, with **−** and **+**.

<Tip>
  Want a walk-through? See [Read from the teleprompter while you record](/tutorials/read-from-the-teleprompter).
</Tip>

## Open it

- On the recording screen, press **Teleprompter** (under **Start recording**).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/record-start.png" alt="Start recording marked 1, with the Teleprompter button under it marked 2" />
</Frame>

- Or, while you record, press the **T** button on the floating recording controls.

## Add your script

The first time, it opens on the script editor. Type or paste what you want to say. Leave an empty line between paragraphs. At the bottom it shows how many words you have and about how long they take to read.

Press **✓** (Done) to see it as a teleprompter. Press the **pencil** to edit it again.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter-edit.png" alt="The script editor. The script is marked 1, the word count and reading time 2, and Done 3" />
</Frame>

## Read while you record

By default, the teleprompter **starts scrolling when the recording starts**, pauses when you pause, and stops when you stop. Just press **Start recording** and read.

You can also control it yourself:

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Start or pause scrolling | Press the **play** button, or **Space** |
| Go faster or slower | Press **+** or **−** at the bottom, or the **↑** and **↓** keys |
| Go back or forward a line | **←** and **→** |
| Go back to the start | The **back arrow** at the bottom left, or **Home** |
| Move by hand | Scroll with your mouse or trackpad |
| Bigger or smaller text | The **A** buttons at the bottom |

The speed is in **words per minute** (wpm), so changing the text size doesn't change how fast you read. The top of the window shows about how much time is left.

## Put it where you want

Drag the top bar to move it anywhere, and drag an edge to resize it. It opens in the same place next time.

Or press the **layout** button and pick a spot:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter-places.png" alt="The list of quick spots, marked 1" />
</Frame>


- **Under the camera**: top centre, so your eyes stay near the camera while you read.
- **Left side** or **Right side**: a tall column.
- **Bottom**: low and centred.
- **Over my camera bubble**: right on top of your camera preview.

## Settings

Press the **gear** in the teleprompter for:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter-settings.png" alt="The teleprompter settings, with Start, pause and stop with the recording marked 1" />
</Frame>


- **Pace**, **Text size** and **Background** (how see-through it is).
- **Start, pause and stop with the recording**: turn this off to control it only by hand.
- **Mirror the text**: flips it, for teleprompter glass.

<Note>
  Your script stays on your Mac. The teleprompter, the recording controls and your camera bubble are all left out of every recording.
</Note>

## Open the recorder

On the **Projects** screen, press **New recording**. (Shortcut: **Command + N**.)

The big picture at the top shows exactly what will be recorded.

## Choose what to record

Use the buttons on the left of the bottom bar:

| Button | What it records | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Display** | Your whole screen | Most videos. Start here. |
| **Window** | One app window | Showing one app without the rest of your screen |
| **Area** | A box you draw on the screen | Keeping the video focused on one part |
| **Tab** | One browser tab, without the browser's toolbar | Showing a website |
| **Device** | A connected iPhone or iPad screen | Showing a phone app |
| **Camera** | Only your camera | Talking to the camera, with no screen |

<Tip>
  Not sure? Choose **Display**. You can crop the video later if you recorded too much. See [Background, crop and shape](/edit/background-and-shape).
</Tip>

## Add your camera and microphone

On the right of the bottom bar:

- **No camera**: click it and pick your camera if you want your face in the video.
- **B**: a second camera, if you have one. You can skip this.
- **No microphone**: click it and pick your microphone so your voice is recorded.
- **No system audio**: turn this on if you want to record sounds your Mac plays, like a video or app sounds.

Want to pick how your face and screen are arranged? See [Layouts and cameras](/record/layouts-and-cameras).

## Record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Start recording">
    A countdown appears on the screen you're recording, so you have a moment to get ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do your thing">
    Show what you want to show and talk as you go. Pauses and mistakes are fine. Cutaway can remove them later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Stop">
    A small control bar floats on your screen while you record. Press the **stop** button on it. Your new project opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The control bar is never part of your video. It also has:

- **Pause**: take a break, then press it again to keep going.
- **Flag**: mark a moment so you can find it later (shortcut: **Control + Option + F**).
- **Teleprompter**: show your script in a floating window while you talk. Only you see it.
- **Camera bubble**: a small mirror of your camera, so you can check how you look. It's never recorded.

<Tip>
  Start or stop recording from any app: hold **Control + Option + Command** and press **R**.
</Tip>

## Recording options

Click the **gear** at the far right of the bottom bar for more options, like:

- **Countdown**: how long to wait before recording starts.
- **Hide desktop icons** and **Hide notifications**: keep your screen tidy while you record.
- **Teleprompter**: write or paste your script.

## What happens next

When you stop, Cutaway shows your recording and asks how you want your [first cut](/edit/first-cut) to feel.

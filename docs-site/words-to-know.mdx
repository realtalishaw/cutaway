---
title: "Words to know"
description: "Simple meanings for the video words you'll see in Cutaway."
---

You don't need to learn these all at once. Come back here whenever a word is new.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Project">
    One video you're working on. It holds your recordings and all your changes. Your projects are listed on the **Projects** screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recording">
    The video Cutaway captures from your screen or camera. Cutaway never changes or deletes it. Your edits are saved separately, so you can always go back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="First cut">
    The first version of your video that Cutaway puts together for you. It removes long pauses and "um"s. You can change anything in it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Editor">
    The screen where you watch and change your video.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Preview">
    The big picture in the middle of the editor. It shows what your finished video will look like.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Timeline">
    The long strip along the bottom of the editor. It shows your whole video from start (left) to end (right), like a ruler of time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Playhead">
    The thin purple line on the timeline. It marks the moment you're looking at. Click anywhere on the timeline to move it there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Clip">
    One piece of video on the timeline. A project can have one clip or many, one after another.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cut / Split / Trim">
    - **Cut**: remove a part of the video.
    - **Split**: chop a clip into two pieces at the playhead.
    - **Trim**: make a clip shorter by dragging its start or end.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transcript">
    Everything you said, written out as text. Cutaway makes it on your Mac. You can edit your video by editing this text.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Filler words">
    Little words like "um" and "uh" that people say while thinking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Zoom">
    Making part of the screen bigger for a moment, so viewers can see a detail.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Layout">
    How your screen and your camera are arranged in the video. For example, your face in a small bubble in the corner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Captions">
    The words you say, shown as text on the video. Great for people watching without sound.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="B-roll">
    Extra video you show over your main video, like a quick clip of a product, while your voice keeps playing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Thumbnail">
    The small picture people see before they click on your video, for example on YouTube.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Export">
    Turning your project into a finished video file you can share.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MP4">
    The most common type of video file. It plays almost everywhere. If you're not sure, choose MP4.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Resolution (720p, 1080p, 4K)">
    How sharp the video is. Bigger numbers are sharper but make bigger files. **1080p** is a good choice for most videos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Frame rate (FPS)">
    How many pictures per second the video shows. **30 FPS** is great for most videos. **60 FPS** looks extra smooth for fast movement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shorts">
    Short, upright videos (like YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Reels) that fit a phone screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>

</Note>

## What the AI helpers do

With an AI helper connected, Cutaway can:

- make a smarter [first cut](/edit/first-cut), choosing what to keep,
- answer requests in plain words with [Ask AI](/ai/ask-ai), like "make the intro shorter",
- suggest better [thumbnails and titles](/share/thumbnail-and-title).

## How it works

Cutaway doesn't have its own AI. Instead, it uses an AI app you already have on your Mac and are signed in to:

- **Claude Code** (from Anthropic), or
- **Codex** (from OpenAI).

There's no extra account or key to set up in Cutaway.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Claude Code or Codex">
    Follow the install steps on the Claude Code or Codex website. You need one, not both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    In Cutaway, open **Settings** (press **Command + ,**), then go to **Processing**. Under **AI on this Mac**, press **Sign in to Claude Code…** or **Sign in to Codex…**. A Terminal window opens. Follow the steps there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which AI to use">
    In the same place, pick your AI app under **AI app**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your privacy

Before Cutaway sends anything to an AI, it shows you a window that says exactly what will be sent. You decide, for each project.

- **What's sent**: the text of what you said, a description of your edits, and a few still pictures from your video.
- **Never sent**: your video and sound files. They always stay on your Mac.

If you say no, or you're not signed in, Cutaway simply uses its built-in rules instead.

<Tip>
  You can see and change these choices any time in **Settings → Processing → What leaves this Mac**.
</Tip>

## Let your AI tool drive Cutaway

Already use Claude Code or Codex for other work? You can also ask it to make videos in Cutaway for you. See [Use Cutaway from Claude or Codex](/ai/make-videos-from-claude-or-codex).

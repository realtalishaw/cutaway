**Ask AI** is a chat inside the editor. Tell it what you want changed, and it suggests edits you can accept or skip.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-chat.png" alt="The Ask AI panel with suggestion buttons and a box to type a request" />
</Frame>

## Ask for a change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Ask AI">
    Click the **speech bubble** in the right sidebar, or press **Command + 9**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type what you want">
    Use your own words. For example:
    - "Tighten the intro"
    - "Fewer zooms, calmer overall"
    - "Zoom in when I open settings"
    - "Where does it drag?"

    Or click one of the suggestions. Press **Return** to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the suggestions">
    The AI answers with **cards**. Each card is one change, with a short reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or skip">
    Press **Apply** on the cards you like, or **Apply all**. Press **Dismiss** to skip one.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Every change is a version

Each change you apply is saved as a **version**. Changed your mind? Open **History** (the clock icon) and go back. See [Undo and versions](/edit/undo-and-versions).

## Without an AI helper

Ask AI works best with an [AI helper](/ai/set-up-ai) connected. Without one, it still understands a few simple requests, such as "less movement", "no zooms", "more movement", "tighter", "slower" and "keep this".

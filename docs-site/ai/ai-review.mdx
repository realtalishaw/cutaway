The **AI review** panel lists everything the [first cut](/edit/first-cut) changed. It's a quick way to check that nothing important was removed.

Click the **sparkle** icon in the right sidebar.

## What you'll see

- **A summary** at the top: who made the first cut (for example **Assembled with Claude**, **Assembled with Codex** or **Assembled with built-in rules**), how many seconds were removed, and a short note about the cut.
- **Flagged while recording**: moments you flagged while recording. See [Flagged moments](#flagged-moments) below.
- **Your call**: ideas Cutaway won't apply without you. See [Your call](#your-call) below.
- **Less certain**: changes Cutaway wasn't sure about. Check these first.
- **All decisions**: every change, like "Pause shortened" or "Filler removed", with the time it happened.

Each change shows two times: where it is in your recording, and where it lands in the finished video. If that part was cut, it says **removed**.

## Check and undo a change

1. Click a change in the list. The playhead jumps there, so you can watch it.
2. Don't like it? Press **Restore**. That part comes back.

## Your call

Some ideas need you to decide, like whether to drop a retake. They appear under **Your call**, each with a short reason.

- Press **Play it** to watch that moment first.
- Press **Apply** to make the change. For a retake, the button says **Remove retake**.
- Press **Dismiss** to say no. For a retake, the button says **Keep both**, so both takes stay in.

## Flagged moments

If you flagged moments while recording (with **Control + Option + F**), each one shows up as a **Flagged moment** card. Click it to jump the playhead there.

The first cut keeps flagged moments. If one has since been cut, the card says so. Press **Remove** to delete the flag. Your video doesn't change.

## Animation ideas

Cutaway also listens to what you say and suggests [animations](/edit/animations) that fit:

- "subscribe" → a **Subscribe** button, timed so the click lands as you say it
- "my name is…" → a **Name tag** with your name
- a number like "10,000 users" → a **Counter** that counts up to it
- "step two…" or "next up…" → a **Title card**
- "click here" → an **Arrow** (drag its box over what to click)

When there are ideas, a note at the top of AI review says so. Press **Show them** to turn them into cards, then press **Add animation** on the ones you want, or **Dismiss** the rest. Each one is added at the moment you said it, and **Command + Z** takes it back out.

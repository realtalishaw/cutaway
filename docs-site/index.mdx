## What is Cutaway?

Cutaway is a Mac app for making videos, like a how-to video for YouTube.

You do three things:

1. **Record.** Cutaway records your screen, and your camera if you want.
2. **Let Cutaway make a first cut.** It removes long pauses and "um"s for you, and puts everything together.
3. **Export.** You get a video file you can upload anywhere.

That's it. You can change anything you like in between, but you don't have to.

## Good to know

- **Your videos stay on your Mac.** Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **The AI helpers are optional.** Cutaway works without them. If you want them, see [Set up the AI helpers](/ai/set-up-ai).
- **You can't break anything.** Every change can be undone, and Cutaway never deletes your original recording.

## What is Cutaway?

Cutaway is a free Mac app for making videos, like a how-to video for YouTube. It records your screen and your camera, cuts out the boring bits for you, and gets everything ready to post.

You do three things:

1. **Record.** Cutaway records your screen, and one or two cameras if you want.
2. **Let Cutaway make a first cut.** It removes long pauses and "um"s, adds zooms and switches between your screen and your face.
3. **Export.** You get a video file, or a whole YouTube package with a thumbnail, title, chapters and captions.

That's it. You can change anything you like in between, but you don't have to.

<Note>
  **Never edited a video before?** You're in the right place. These docs explain every step in plain words, with pictures. If you see a word you don't know, check [Words to know](/words-to-know).
</Note>

## Good to know

- **It's free and open source.** The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/realtalishaw/cutaway).
- **Your videos stay on your Mac.** Recording, editing and exporting all happen on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
- **The AI helpers are optional.** Cutaway works without them. If you want them, see [Set up the AI helpers](/ai/set-up-ai).
- **You can't break anything.** Every change can be undone, and Cutaway never deletes your original recording.

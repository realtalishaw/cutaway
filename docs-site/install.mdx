## What you need

- A Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer). To check, click the Apple menu in the top-left corner, then **About This Mac**. Look for "Chip: Apple M…".
- macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer.

## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the app">
    Download **Cutaway.app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it to Applications">
    Drag **Cutaway** into your **Applications** folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Double-click **Cutaway** in Applications. You'll see the **Projects** screen.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/screens/library-empty.png" alt="The Projects screen with Import and New recording buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Let Cutaway record your screen

Your Mac protects your screen, camera and microphone. The first time you record, macOS asks if Cutaway may use them. Cutaway only asks for the things you switch on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Allow… in Cutaway">
    When you start your first recording, Cutaway shows an **Allow…** button. Press it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn Cutaway on in System Settings">
    macOS opens **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording**. Find **Cutaway** in the list and switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Come back to Cutaway">
    Switch back to Cutaway. It notices the change by itself. You don't need to restart it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Camera and microphone work the same way. When macOS asks, click **Allow**.
</Tip>

Something not working? See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).

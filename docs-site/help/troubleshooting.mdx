---
title: "Troubleshooting"
description: "Quick fixes for common problems."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="macOS says it can't verify Cutaway">
    This is normal the first time you open Cutaway. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Cutaway. You only do this once.

    See [Install Cutaway](/install#open-cutaway-for-the-first-time) for the full steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The recorder says it can't record my screen">
    Your Mac needs to give Cutaway permission.

    1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording**.
    2. Find **Cutaway** and switch it on.
    3. Go back to Cutaway. You don't need to restart it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My camera or microphone doesn't show up">
    1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, then **Camera** (or **Microphone**).
    2. Make sure **Cutaway** is switched on.
    3. Check your camera or mic is plugged in. Then close and reopen the recorder.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My iPhone doesn't show up as a camera">
    - Use the same Apple Account on your iPhone and Mac.
    - Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on both.
    - Lock your iPhone and keep it close to your Mac, or plug it in with a cable.

    See [Use your iPhone as a camera](/record/layouts-and-cameras#use-your-iphone-as-a-camera).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There's no sound in my recording">
    Before recording, check the microphone menu in the bottom bar doesn't say **No microphone**. Pick your microphone there.

    Want the sound from your Mac too (like a video playing)? Turn on **system audio** in the same bar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cutaway cut out something I wanted">
    Nothing is deleted for good.

    - Open the **AI review** panel (sparkle icon) and press **Restore** on that change.
    - Or open the **Transcript**, select the crossed-out words, and press **Restore**.
    - Or press **Command + Z** to undo.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My camera and screen are out of sync">
    Your lips don't match your voice? Click **Sources** in the top bar and press **Review sync** next to your camera. Press **Find sync from audio** to let Cutaway line them up, or change the **Offset** yourself until your lips match.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My screen recording was imported as a camera">
    Click **Sources** in the top bar. Use the menu next to the file to change it to **Screen**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The AI helpers aren't working">
    - Check you have **Claude Code** or **Codex** installed and signed in. See [Set up the AI helpers](/ai/set-up-ai).
    - In **Settings → Processing**, press **Sign in to Claude Code…** or **Sign in to Codex…** if Cutaway says you're signed out.
    - No AI? No problem. Cutaway still works with its built-in rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cutaway quit while I was recording">
    Your footage is saved as you record, so most of it is safe.

    1. Open Cutaway. On the **Projects** screen, look for **Interrupted work**.
    2. Press **Recover** to add the footage to its project. Everything up to the last save point comes back.
    3. Don't need it? Press **Discard** instead.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't find my exported video">
    Unless you picked somewhere else, it's in the **Cutaway Exports** folder inside your **Movies** folder.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

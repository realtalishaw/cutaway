## Where your work is saved

- **Projects**: in the **Cutaway** folder inside your **Movies** folder.
- **Exports**: in the **Cutaway Exports** folder inside your **Movies** folder.

You can change both under **Storage**. Press **Change…** next to **Project library** (for projects) or **YouTube packages** (for exports).

<Note>
  Changing **Project library** only affects new projects. Projects you already have stay where they are.
</Note>

## When you import a file

Under **Storage → Imported media**:

- **Copy into the project** (recommended): your project keeps working even if you move or delete the original file. Uses more space.
- **Leave it where it is**: saves space, but the project needs the original file to stay put.

## Online background photos

Also under **Storage**, **Online background photos** lets the Background tab search Unsplash for photos. It's optional. You need a free key from Unsplash: paste it into **Unsplash access key** and press **Save**. Only your search words are sent to Unsplash.

## Free up space

Under **Storage → Derived caches**, you can see how much space a project's stills, previews and analysis use. Press **Clear derived caches** to delete them. Your recordings, edit and thumbnails are safe. Cutaway makes the caches again when it needs them.

<Tip>
  Open a project first. The cache size only shows for the project you have open.
</Tip>

## Processing

**Processing** is where you set up AI and speech-to-text.

- **AI on this Mac**: choose which **AI app** Cutaway uses: **Automatic**, **Claude Code** or **Codex**. You can also set which model each one uses, and where it's installed. Most people leave these as they are. See [Set up AI](/ai/set-up-ai).
- **Playbooks**: the guidance Cutaway gives the AI for **Editing**, **Titles** and **Thumbnails**. Press **Read playbook** to see it. Type in **Add your own notes** (for example, "always sentence case") and press **Save notes**.
- **What leaves this Mac**: exactly what is sent to the AI company, what is never sent, and how long it's kept.
- **Transcription**: how your speech is turned into text. This always happens on your Mac. Pick an **Engine**: **Automatic**, **Apple on-device speech** or **Whisper model file**. **Automatic** is best for most people. Set **Spoken language** to the language you speak in your videos.
- **Analytics**: Cutaway collects no analytics. There's nothing to turn off.

## AI tools

**AI tools** lets Claude Code or Codex make videos in Cutaway for you.

1. Turn on **Let AI tools control Cutaway**.
2. Press **Connect** next to Claude Code or Codex. If it's already connected, the button says **Connect again**.

Using a different AI app? **Add it by hand** shows a command you can copy into it. See [Use Cutaway from Claude or Codex](/ai/make-videos-from-claude-or-codex).

## Recording

- **Countdown**: how long Cutaway waits after you press record: **None**, **3 seconds**, **5 seconds** or **10 seconds**.
- **Global shortcut**: **Control + Option + Command + R** starts or stops a recording from any app.
- **What a recording keeps**: a reminder of what Cutaway saves. For example, your screen, cameras and sound are saved as separate files, so you can edit each one.

## Accessibility

- **Reduce motion in the app**: turns off movement in Cutaway's own screens. Your video's zooms and transitions don't change.
- **Transcript text size**: makes the words in the Transcript panel bigger or smaller.

## Shortcuts

Click any shortcut, then press the new keys. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Presets

A **preset** saves a look: background, frame, cursor, camera and caption style, motion and pacing. It never saves your cuts.

1. Open a project you like the look of.
2. Under **Save the current look**, type a name and press **Save preset**.

Under **Your presets**:

- **Apply** gives the open project that look.
- **Make default** uses it for every new project.
- The **trash** button deletes the preset.

## About

**About** shows which version of Cutaway you have. **What is not here yet** lists things Cutaway can't do yet, like share links or recording HDR.

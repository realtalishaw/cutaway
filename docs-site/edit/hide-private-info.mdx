---
title: "Hide private info"
description: "Blur or cover emails, passwords and anything else you don't want people to see."
---

Did your recording show an email address, a password or a bank number? You can blur it out.

## Blur part of the screen

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the moment">
    Click the timeline where the private info first shows up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a blur">
    In the tools on the left of the preview, click the **blur** button (it says "Hide sensitive content" when you hover over it).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag over the private info">
    Drag a box on the preview over the part you want to hide. Press **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it last long enough">
    A pink **Blur** block appears on the timeline. Drag its ends so it covers the whole time the info is on screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

### Blur styles

- **Blur**: makes it fuzzy and unreadable.
- **Cover**: hides it with a solid box.
- **Spotlight**: the opposite. Everything *except* the box is darkened, to draw attention to it.

<Tip>
  The blur moves with your screen when you zoom, so the info stays hidden.
</Tip>

## Let Cutaway find private info

Cutaway can look for things like email addresses, passwords, keys and card numbers for you.

Click the **shield** in the tools on the left of the preview, then press **Scan the recording**. Cutaway shows what it found, and you choose what to blur.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/find-sensitive.png" alt="The Find sensitive data window with a Scan the recording button" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  The scan happens on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere. Always watch your video before sharing, too, in case something was missed.
</Note>

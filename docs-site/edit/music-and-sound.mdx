## Make your voice clear

**Voice cleanup** is on by default. It removes low rumbling noise and keeps your voice at an even volume.

To hear the difference, switch on **Bypass to compare**. Switch it off again when you're done.

<Note>
  Recorded with two microphones (for example, a camera *and* a mic)? Cutaway plays only one voice at a time so you don't hear an echo. Pick which one under **Voice**.
</Note>

## Change the volume

Use the sliders under **Levels** to make each track louder or quieter.

## Add background music

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press the + on the audio row">
    It's at the end of the bottom row of the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Music or sound from my files…">
    Pick a music file from your Mac.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The music appears as a green block on the timeline. Cutaway automatically makes it quieter while you're talking, so your voice is always easy to hear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/add-menu-audio.png" alt="The add audio menu with Music or sound from my files, Record voiceover, and Built-in sound at the playhead" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Only use music you have the right to use. Most popular songs are not allowed on YouTube without permission. Look for "royalty-free" music.
</Warning>

### Change the music

Click the music block on the timeline to select it. Then you can:

- **Drag it** to start the music at a different moment.
- **Drag its ends** to make it shorter.
- Press **Loop** to start the song again when it runs out.
- Change how it **fades** in and out.

## Add a sound effect

Move the playhead to the moment, press the **+** on the audio row, and choose **Built-in sound at the playhead**. Cutaway has a few built-in sounds, like a click, pop and whoosh.

## Record a voiceover

A **voiceover** is your voice recorded on its own, played over your video. Press the **+** on the audio row and choose **Record voiceover…**.

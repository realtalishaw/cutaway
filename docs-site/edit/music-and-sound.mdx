## Make your voice clear

**Voice cleanup** is on by default. It removes low rumbling noise and keeps your voice at an even volume.

To hear the difference, switch on **Bypass to compare**. Switch it off again when you're done.

<Note>
  Recorded with two microphones (for example, a camera *and* a mic)? Cutaway plays only one voice at a time so you don't hear an echo. Pick which one under **Voice**.
</Note>

## Change the volume

Use the sliders under **Levels** to make each track louder or quieter.

## Add background music

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press the + on the audio row">
    It's at the end of the bottom row of the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the music comes from">
    - **Music or sound from my files…** to pick a music file on your Mac.
    - **Generate music…** to have AI make some. See [Make voice, music and pictures with AI](/edit/ai-generation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The music appears as a green block on the audio row, with its waveform. Cutaway makes it quieter while you're talking, so your voice is always easy to hear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/add-menu-audio.png" alt="The add audio menu" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Only use music you have the right to use. Most popular songs are not allowed on YouTube without permission. Look for "royalty-free" music.
</Warning>

### Change the music

Click the music block on the timeline to select it. Then you can:

- **Drag it** to start the music at a different moment.
- **Drag its ends** to make it shorter or longer.
- **Split** it at the playhead (press **S** or **Command + B**) to cut it into two pieces you can move or delete separately.
- Turn on **Loop** to start the song again when it runs out, so a short piece plays under the whole video.
- Set **Fade in** and **Fade out**.
- Set its **Level**, and turn **Lower under speech** on or off.

Music is placed on the *finished* video, so cutting parts of your recording never chops the music.

### Make the music louder or quieter at certain moments

Under **Volume over time**, move the playhead to a moment and press the button to **add a point** there. Then, on the music block:

- **Drag a point** up or down to change the volume. Drag it all the way down to silence the music there.
- Hold **Shift** while dragging to keep the point at the same moment.
- **Double-click a point** to remove it.

## Add a sound effect

Move the playhead to the moment, press the **+** on the audio row, and choose:

- **Built-in sound at the playhead**: a click, pop, whoosh and more. These are free and made on your Mac.
- **Generate a sound effect…**: describe any sound and AI makes it. See [Make voice, music and pictures with AI](/edit/ai-generation#make-music-or-a-sound-effect).
- **Music or sound from my files…**: use a sound file you already have.

<Tip>
  In the **Audio** panel, under **Sound effects**, you can add a sound **On every cut**, **On every view change**, or **As every text appears**, all in one step.
</Tip>

## Record a voiceover

A **voiceover** is your voice recorded on its own and played over your video.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Put the playhead where you want to start talking">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press + on the audio row and choose Record voiceover…">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your microphone and press the record button">
    The window shows how loud you are and how long you've been talking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press stop, then add it">
    Listen to the take. Keep it to put it on the audio row where the playhead was, or try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Have AI read it for you

Don't want to record your own voice? **AI voiceover…** (also on the audio row's **+**) reads out what you type, with a voice from **ElevenLabs**, **fal.ai**, or your Mac's own voices (free). See [Make a voiceover](/edit/ai-generation#make-a-voiceover).

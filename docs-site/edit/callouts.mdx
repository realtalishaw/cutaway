A **callout** is a small label that points out something on your screen, like "Click here" or "This is the important part".

</Frame>

## Add a callout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the moment">
    Click the timeline where the callout should appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Callout** in the **Add** bar on the left of the preview (**1** in the picture). A green **Callout** block appears on the timeline (**2**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your words">
    In the panel on the right, type in **Text** (**3**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Press **Click the preview to place it** (**4**), then click the spot on the preview.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Other styles

At the top of the panel you can switch between:

- **Callout**: a small label.
- **Section**: a banner to start a new part of your video.
- **Lower third**: a label near the bottom of the screen.

## Add a sound

Under **Sound as it appears**, pick a little sound effect like **Pop**, **Ding** or **Whoosh**. It plays when the callout shows up.

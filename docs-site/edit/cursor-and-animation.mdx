## Cursor

The **cursor** is your mouse pointer. When you record in Cutaway, it keeps the cursor separate from the video, so you can change it afterwards.

Click the **Cursor** icon (the arrow) in the right sidebar, or press **Command + 2**. You can:

- change the **Cursor size**,
- turn on **Smooth movement** so it glides instead of jittering,
- **Hide cursor if not moving**, or **Hide cursor** completely,
- add a **Click effect**: a soft ripple every time you click.

## Keyboard shortcuts on screen

Teaching keyboard shortcuts? Cutaway can show the keys you press on the video, like **⌘ C**.

1. Before you record, open the recording options (the gear in the recorder) and turn on **Record keyboard shortcuts**.
2. In the editor, click the **⌘** icon in the right sidebar and turn on **Show keyboard shortcuts**.

## Animation speed

Click the **Animations** icon in the right sidebar (or press **Command + 8**).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-motion.png" alt="The Animation panel with Slow, Medium and Rapid speed, zoom level and motion blur" />
</Frame>

- **Animation speed**: how fast zooms and layout changes move. **Slow**, **Medium** or **Rapid**.
- **Default zoom level**: how far new zooms go in.
- **Motion blur**: a little blur while things move, so it looks smoother.

## Animated graphics

Press the **+** on the top row of the timeline and choose **Animation…** to add a moving graphic, like an animated title or arrow. Pick one from **Templates**.

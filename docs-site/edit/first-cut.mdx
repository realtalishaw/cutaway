---

After you record, Cutaway can make a **first cut**: a ready-to-watch version of your video. It:

- removes long pauses where nothing happens,
- removes filler words like "um" and "uh",
- switches between your screen and your face at good moments,
- adds zooms when you show something small.

You can change anything it does afterwards.

## Make a first cut

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/first-cut-brief.png" alt="The first cut screen: who it's for, length, pacing, movement, and the Create first cut button" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer a few questions (or don't)">
    The choices already selected work well. Change only what you care about:

    - **Who is it for?** For example, "people learning this for the first time".
    - **Length**: keep it **Natural**, or aim for about 3, 5, 8 or 12 minutes.
    - **Pacing**: **Relaxed** keeps more pauses. **Energetic** is snappier.
    - **Movement**: **Calm** means fewer zooms. **Lively** means more.
    - **Anything else?** Write any wishes in your own words. This is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Create first cut">
    Cutaway works for a little while, then opens your video in the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Prefer to do it all yourself? Press **Skip and edit by hand**.
</Note>

## Make a new first cut

Changed your mind about the pacing? Press **First cut** in the top bar, change your choices, and press **Create a new first cut**.

Your own changes are kept. Cutaway saves your current version first, so you can always go back. See [Undo and versions](/edit/undo-and-versions).

## With or without AI

The first cut works fine without any AI. Cutaway uses its own built-in rules, and nothing leaves your Mac.

If you [set up the AI helpers](/ai/set-up-ai), Cutaway can also ask Claude or Codex for smarter choices, like which parts to keep. It always asks you first before sending anything.

## See what changed

Open the **AI review** panel (the sparkle icon in the sidebar) to see every change the first cut made, and put back anything you want. See [AI review](/ai/ai-review).

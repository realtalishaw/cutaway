---
title: "Edit by transcript"
description: "Cut your video by deleting words, just like editing a document."
---

Cutaway writes out everything you said. This is called the **transcript**. Delete words from it, and that part of the video is removed too.

It's the easiest way to cut out mistakes.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-transcript.png" alt="The Transcript panel showing what was said, with one word highlighted" />
</Frame>

## Open the transcript

Click the **Transcript** icon (the lines) in the right sidebar. Or press **Command + 5**.

<Note>
  The transcript is made on your Mac. Your voice is never sent anywhere.
</Note>

## Remove words

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the words">
    Click the first word you want to remove, and drag to the last one. They're highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut them">
    Press **Backspace**, or click **Cut** in the bar that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    Removed words are shown crossed out. Press **Space** to play and hear the result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Put words back

Select the crossed-out words and click **Restore**.

## Clean up "um"s and pauses in one click

If Cutaway finds any, two buttons appear at the top of the transcript:

- **Remove filler words**: cuts "um" and "uh". The words around them stay.
- **Tighten pauses**: shortens silent gaps where nothing happens on screen.

<Note>
  Already made a first cut? It usually removed these for you, so the buttons may not show.
</Note>

## Other things you can do

- **Click a word** to jump to that moment in the video.
- **Fix spelling**: select one word (or double-click it) and click **Fix spelling**. This only changes the caption text. No video is removed.
- **Keep**: select words and click **Keep** to protect them. The AI helpers will never cut them.

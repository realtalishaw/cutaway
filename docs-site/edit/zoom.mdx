---

A **zoom** makes part of your screen bigger for a few seconds, then goes back. It's the best way to help people see small buttons or text.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/add-zoom.png" alt="A zoom added on the timeline, with the Zoom panel open on the right" />
</Frame>

## Add a zoom

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the moment">
    Click the timeline where you want the zoom to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the zoom">
    Click the **magnifying glass** at the top of the tools on the left of the preview. A purple zoom block appears on the timeline. It lasts 3 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where to zoom">
    In the Zoom panel, click **Click the preview to set the target**. Then click the spot on the preview you want to zoom into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it">
    Press **Space** to watch it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change a zoom

Click the zoom block on the timeline to select it. Then:

- **Make it longer or shorter**: drag the ends of the block.
- **Move it**: drag the whole block.
- **Zoom in more or less**: use the **Magnification** slider.
- **Zoom style**:
  - **Zoom in**: the whole video moves in. The usual choice.
  - **Magnifier**: a round magnifying glass appears over one spot.
  - **Instant**: jumps in with no movement.
- **Delete it**: press **Backspace**, or click **Delete** in the bar above the preview.

<Tip>
  Recorded your screen in Cutaway? Turn on **Follow the cursor** and the zoom follows your mouse pointer.
</Tip>

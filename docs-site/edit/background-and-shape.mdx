## Background

Cutaway puts your screen recording on a colorful background with rounded corners, so it looks polished.

Click the **Background** icon in the right sidebar (or press **Command + 1**).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-background.png" alt="The Background panel with wallpaper choices, blur, padding and rounded corners" />
</Frame>

- **Wallpaper**: pick one of the ready-made pictures. Or press **Pick random wallpaper**.
- **Gradient**: a smooth blend between two colors. Drag **Angle** to turn the direction of the blend.
- **Color**: one plain color. Click **Custom colour** (the last box) to pick any color you like.
- **Image**: a picture from your Mac (press **Choose an image…**), or a free photo from the internet (see below).

Below that:

- **Background blur**: makes the background softer.
- **Padding**: how much background shows around your screen. Less padding means a bigger screen.
- **Rounded corners**: how round the corners of your screen are.
- **Shadow**: a soft shadow under your screen.
- **Inset**: a thin light edge around your screen.

### Find a background photo online

Pick **Image**, then use **Search photos** to find a free photo from Unsplash. Type a word like "mountains" and click a photo to use it.

<Note>
  Search needs a free Unsplash key first. Get one at unsplash.com/developers, then paste it into **Unsplash access key** under **Settings** → **Storage** → **Online background photos**. Only your search words are sent to Unsplash. Nothing from your project is.
</Note>

## Screen look

The **Screen look** group at the bottom of the Background panel changes the colors and sharpness of your screen recording:

- **Screen LUT**: a color recipe file from another app. Press **Choose a .cube file…** to pick one, then drag **Strength** to make it stronger or softer. The file is copied into your project.
- **Screen sharpening**: drag **Amount** to make your screen a little crisper.

## Crop

**Cropping** cuts away the edges of your recording, so only the part you want shows. For example, to hide your Mac's menu bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Crop">
    It's above the top-left corner of the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag the edges">
    Drag the corners and edges of the box. Only what's inside the box will appear in the video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press the purple button at the bottom to keep your crop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/crop.png" alt="The Crop window. The crop box is marked 1, the shape choices 2, Hide browser bar 3, and the button that saves the crop 4" />
</Frame>

### Hide the browser's tabs and address bar

Recorded a browser window, and its tabs, address bar or side bar are in the video? Cutaway can find them for you.

1. Press **Crop**.
2. Press **Hide browser bar**. Cutaway looks through your whole recording, finds the browser's bars, and moves the box to just the page. It handles tabs on the top or down the side, and a browser that turns dark on dark pages.
3. Check the picture, drag an edge if you need to, and press **Apply crop**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/crop-browser-bar.png" alt="Hide browser bar pressed, marked 1. The crop box, marked 2, now starts below the browser's address bar. Apply crop is marked 3" />
</Frame>

Watch it step by step: [Hide your browser's tabs and address bar](/tutorials/hide-browser-bar).

<Tip>
  Next time, record with **Tab** instead of **Window** in the recording bar. Then the tabs and address bar are left out from the start. See [Start a recording](/record/start-a-recording#tab-keep-the-tab-in-front).
</Tip>

## Video shape

The button above the preview (it says **Wide 16:9**) sets the shape of your whole video.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/output-shape.png" alt="The video shape menu open from the Wide 16:9 button, marked 1" />
</Frame>

- **Wide 16:9**: the normal shape for YouTube and computer screens. Use this if you're not sure.
- **Vertical 9:16**: tall, for phones (Shorts, TikTok, Reels).
- **Square 1:1**: for some social feeds.
- **Classic 4:3** and **Tall 4:5**: other shapes some sites like.

<Tip>
  Want upright Shorts from a wide video? Use the [Shorts](/share/shorts) page. It makes upright copies and leaves your main video alone.
</Tip>

## Presets

Found a look you love? Press **Presets** in the top bar to save this project's look, and use it again on your next video.

## Background

Cutaway puts your screen recording on a colorful background with rounded corners, so it looks polished.

Click the **Background** icon in the right sidebar (or press **Command + 1**).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-background.png" alt="The Background panel with wallpaper choices, blur, padding and rounded corners" />
</Frame>

- **Wallpaper**: pick one of the ready-made pictures. Or press **Pick random wallpaper**.
- **Gradient**: a smooth blend between two colors.
- **Color**: one plain color.
- **Image**: a picture from your Mac.

Below that:

- **Background blur**: makes the background softer.
- **Padding**: how much background shows around your screen. Less padding means a bigger screen.
- **Rounded corners**: how round the corners of your screen are.
- **Shadow**: a soft shadow under your screen.

## Crop

**Cropping** cuts away the edges of your recording, so only the part you want shows. For example, to hide your Mac's menu bar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Crop">
    It's above the top-left corner of the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag the edges">
    Drag the corners and edges of the box. Only what's inside the box will appear in the video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press the purple button at the bottom to keep your crop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

</Frame>

## Video shape

The button above the preview (it says **Wide 16:9**) sets the shape of your whole video.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/output-shape.png" alt="The video shape menu" />
</Frame>

- **Wide 16:9**: the normal shape for YouTube and computer screens. Use this if you're not sure.
- **Vertical 9:16**: tall, for phones (Shorts, TikTok, Reels).
- **Square 1:1**: for some social feeds.
- **Classic 4:3** and **Tall 4:5**: other shapes some sites like.

<Tip>
  Want upright Shorts from a wide video? Use the [Shorts](/share/shorts) page. It makes upright copies and leaves your main video alone.
</Tip>

## Presets

Found a look you love? Press **Presets** in the top bar to save this project's look, and use it again on your next video.

The **editor** is where you watch and change your video. It looks busy at first, but there are only six parts.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/editor-tour.png" alt="The editor with six numbered areas: 1 top bar, 2 preview, 3 Add bar, 4 play controls, 5 timeline, 6 sidebar" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Top bar">
    Switch between pages: **Sources** (your files), **Edit**, **Package** (thumbnail and title) and **Shorts**.

    On the left, the **folder** button takes you back to your other projects. The **trash** button is **Move project to Trash**. Cutaway asks first, and you can still get the project back from the Trash.

    In the middle is the project's name. Click it (a small pencil shows when you point at it) to rename the project, then press **Return** (or **Esc** to keep the old name). The project's folder is renamed too.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/rename-title.png" alt="The project name in the middle of the top bar, marked 1" />
    </Frame>

    You can also rename a project from your projects list: open the **…** menu on its card (**1** below), or right-click it, and choose **Rename…** (**2**).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/library-menu.png" alt="A project card with its … menu open. The … button is marked 1 and Rename… is marked 2" />
    </Frame>

    On the right are **Settings** (the gear), the **Command menu** (or press **Command + K**), **Undo** and **Redo**, **Presets** (save and reuse a look), **First cut** and **Export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    The big picture. It shows exactly what your finished video will look like at this moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add bar">
    The bar marked **Add**, on the left of the preview, puts things into your video at the playhead:

    1. **Zoom**: a [zoom](/edit/zoom).
    2. **View**: change what shows from here: camera only, screen + camera, split screen, screen only, or the other camera. See [Camera layouts](/edit/camera-layouts).
    3. **Hide**: **Blur an area**, or **Find private info** like emails and passwords. See [Hide private info](/edit/hide-private-info).
    4. **Callout**: a [callout](/edit/callouts).
    5. **Text**: [text](/edit/text-and-titles).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/add-bar.png" alt="The Add bar with its five buttons numbered: Zoom, View, Hide, Callout and Text" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play controls">
    **Play** and **pause** (or press the **Space** bar). The arrows next to it jump to the previous or next cut. The scissors on the left [cut out a part](/edit/timeline-and-clips#cut-out-a-part).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timeline">
    Your whole video from start (left) to end (right). The purple line is the **playhead**: the moment you're looking at. Click anywhere to move it. Zooms, text and [animations](/edit/animations) show as blocks on the top row, your video in the middle, and music at the bottom. Read more in [Timeline and clips](/edit/timeline-and-clips).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sidebar">
    The icons down the right side open panels: background, cursor, camera, captions, transcript, audio, text, animations, Ask AI, AI review and history. They change how the whole video looks, or the item you selected. They don't add anything at the playhead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The sidebar panels

| Icon | Panel | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | **Background** | The color or picture behind your video, and its corners. [More](/edit/background-and-shape) |
| Arrow | **Cursor** | How the mouse pointer looks. [More](/edit/cursor-and-animation) |
| Camera | **Camera** | The shape, size and look of your camera. [More](/edit/camera-layouts) |
| Captions | **Captions** | Words on screen. [More](/edit/captions) |
| Lines | **Transcript** | Edit by text. [More](/edit/edit-by-transcript) |
| Speaker | **Audio** | Voice, volume and music. [More](/edit/music-and-sound) |
| T | **Text and titles** | Titles and name labels. [More](/edit/text-and-titles) |
| ⌘ | **Keyboard shortcuts** | Show the keys you pressed. [More](/edit/cursor-and-animation#keyboard-shortcuts-on-screen) |
| Path | **Animations** | How fast zooms move. [More](/edit/cursor-and-animation#animation-speed) |
| Speech bubble | **Ask AI** | Ask for changes in plain words. [More](/ai/ask-ai) |
| Sparkle | **AI review** | What the first cut changed. [More](/ai/ai-review) |
| Clock | **History** | Earlier versions of your video. [More](/edit/undo-and-versions) |

<Tip>
  Hover over any button to see what it does.
</Tip>

## Simple and Advanced timeline

Next to the play controls is an **Advanced** switch. Leave it off while you're learning. The simple timeline shows your text and effects, your video, and your music.

Turn **Advanced** on to see every layer separately, such as layouts, zooms, blurs and speed changes. See [Simple and Advanced timeline](/edit/timeline-and-clips#simple-and-advanced-timeline).

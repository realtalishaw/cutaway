---
title: "Edit with two cameras"
description: "See both camera angles, switch between them, and cut your video when you recorded with two cameras."
---

If you recorded with two cameras, for example your Mac's camera as **Camera A** and your iPhone as **Camera B**, both are in your project. Each one recorded the whole take, in sync with your screen and your voice.

## How it works

You don't edit each camera separately. There's one video, and at every moment you choose **what shows**: your screen, a camera, or both, and **which camera**.

- **Cutting is shared.** When you cut out a part, it's cut from your screen, both cameras and your voice at once, so everything stays in sync.
- **Views choose the camera.** A **view** is a stretch of time that says what shows, like "Camera B, full screen" or "Screen + Camera A in a bubble". The first cut adds views for you, switching between your cameras.
- **Outside a view, the main camera shows.** That's Camera A unless you change it.

## See Camera B

The fastest way to look at your Camera B footage:

1. Open the **Camera** tab on the right (**Command + 3**).
2. Under **Your two cameras**, pick **Camera B**.

Now Camera B shows everywhere, including in every view, so you can play through and watch it. Pick **Camera A** to switch back. Both choices can be undone with **Command + Z**.

The panel also tells you how many of your views show Camera B right now.

## Switch cameras at one moment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Move the playhead to the moment">
    Click the timeline or the transcript where you want the other camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch camera">
    In the **Add** bar on the left of the preview, click **View**, then **Other camera (A ↔ B)**. Or press **Control + A**.

    From the playhead, the other camera shows for 4 seconds, in the same arrangement. If only your screen was showing, the other camera shows full screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it longer or shorter">
    Drag the ends of the new block on the timeline (see [Show the views on the timeline](#show-the-views-on-the-timeline) below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### For a longer stretch

1. Select a stretch of time: drag across the video row, or press **I** at the start and **O** at the end.
2. In the panel on the right, under **Show here**, pick **Camera A** or **Camera B** next to **With camera**.
3. Click how it should look: **Screen + camera**, **Camera** or **Split**.

## Show the views on the timeline

Views have their own row on the timeline, which is hidden in the simple timeline. To see it:

1. Switch on **Advanced**, in the controls just above the timeline on the right.
2. Click **1 visible timeline** next to it (or press **Command + T**) so it says **3 visible timelines**.

The **Layout** row now shows each view as a block, for example **Camera B** or **Screen + camera A**. A sparkle means the AI added it.

With a view block you can:

- **Change its camera:** click it, then pick **Camera A** or **Camera B** under **Camera angle** on the right. Or click it and press **Control + A**.
- **Change how it looks:** pick **Screen**, **Both**, **Camera**, **Split** or **Inset**.
- **Move or resize it:** drag it, or drag its ends.
- **Remove it:** click it and press **Delete**. The main camera shows there again.

## Cut while watching Camera B

1. Pick **Camera B** under **Your two cameras** in the **Camera** tab, so you can see it while you work.
2. Cut as usual: select the words in the transcript and press **Delete** (see [Edit by transcript](/edit/edit-by-transcript)), or mark a stretch with **I** and **O** and press **X** (see [Timeline and clips](/edit/timeline-and-clips)).
3. When you're done, pick the camera you want as the main one, and switch individual moments as above.

Every cut applies to both cameras, so you never have to line them up again.

## Make the cameras look alike

Two cameras rarely match. In the **Camera** tab, under **Camera look**, adjust **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation** and **Warmth**. These apply to the camera everywhere in the video.

## Which microphone you hear

If both cameras recorded sound, Cutaway plays one voice at a time so you don't hear an echo. Pick which one in the **Audio** tab (**Command + 6**), under **Voice**. See [Voice, music and sound](/edit/music-and-sound).

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My iPhone's screen went dark while recording. Did it record?">
    Yes. When your iPhone is the camera, its screen goes dark or shows that it's in use. It keeps recording the whole time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I don't see Camera B anywhere">
    Open the **Camera** tab. If you don't see **Your two cameras**, the project has only one camera. Check that you picked a camera in the **B** menu before recording. See [Layouts and cameras](/record/layouts-and-cameras#two-cameras).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Camera B is greyed out for part of my video">
    A camera can only show where it has footage. If one camera started late or was disconnected for a moment, Cutaway shows the other one there instead.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Change the layout for one moment

A **layout** is how your screen and your face are arranged. You can switch layouts during the video. For example, show just your face when you say hello, then switch to your screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the moment">
    Click the timeline where the change should start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a layout from the tools">
    In the tools on the left of the preview, click one of:
    - **Fullscreen camera**: just your face.
    - **Split screen**: screen and face side by side.
    - **Camera overlay**: your screen with your face in a bubble.
    - **Screen only**: just your screen.

    The new layout lasts 4 seconds. Drag its block on the timeline to make it longer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keyboard shortcuts: **Control + L** for camera only, **Control + S** for screen only, **Control + B** for both.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The layout buttons only show when your project has a camera.
</Note>

## Two cameras

If you recorded with two cameras (for example your Mac's camera and your iPhone), both are in your project.

- **Pick the main camera:** in the **Camera** tab on the right, under **Your two cameras**, choose **Camera A** or **Camera B**. It shows everywhere.
- **Switch for one moment:** move the playhead there and press the **switch camera** button in the tools on the left of the preview, or **Control + A**.

For everything about editing with two cameras, see [Edit with two cameras](/edit/two-cameras).

## Make your camera look good

Click the **Camera** icon in the right sidebar (or press **Command + 3**).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-camera.png" alt="The Camera panel with looks, brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth" />
</Frame>

Here you can change:

- **Camera shape, size and roundness**: **Rounded**, **Circle** or **Square**, big or small.
- **Camera position**: which corner the bubble sits in.
- **Camera shadow**: a soft shadow under the camera bubble.
- **Camera look**: quick color styles like **Natural**, **Studio**, **Golden**, **Cool** or **Mono**. Or adjust **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation** and **Warmth** yourself.
- **Camera LUT**: a color recipe file from another app. Press **Choose a .cube file…** to pick one, then drag **Strength** to make it stronger or softer. The file is copied into your project.
- **Camera sharpening**: drag **Amount** to make your camera a little crisper.
- **Zoom on face** and **Follow face**: keeps your face centered and filling the frame.
- **Mirror camera**: flips your camera left to right.

## Remove the background behind you

Under **Remove background**, turn on **Cut out the person**. Cutaway hides what's behind you. This happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

Then pick what goes behind you:

- **None**: you stand right on top of your screen recording, with no camera bubble.
- **Blur**: your own background, blurred.
- **Color**: a plain color. Click the color box to pick one.

## Jump cuts

When you cut a mistake out of a talking-head video, your face can "jump" a little. Cutaway can hide this by zooming your camera in slightly after each cut, so it looks like a deliberate camera change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    In the Camera panel, under **Jump cuts**, turn on **Punch in on jump cuts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how far it zooms">
    Drag **Punch-in** (from 105% to 130%). The panel lists every cut where the framing changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave out a cut (optional)">
    Click a cut in the list (or select it on the timeline). Then turn off **Punch in at this cut** for that one cut.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Punch-in only works where a cut sits inside one full-screen camera shot, with no zoom and no layout change at that moment.
</Note>

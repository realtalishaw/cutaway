## Change the layout for one moment

A **layout** is how your screen and your face are arranged. You can switch layouts during the video. For example, show just your face when you say hello, then switch to your screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the moment">
    Click the timeline where the change should start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a layout from the tools">
    In the tools on the left of the preview, click one of:
    - **Fullscreen camera**: just your face.
    - **Split screen**: screen and face side by side.
    - **Camera overlay**: your screen with your face in a bubble.
    - **Screen only**: just your screen.

    The new layout lasts 4 seconds. Drag its block on the timeline to make it longer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keyboard shortcuts: **Control + L** for camera only, **Control + S** for screen only, **Control + B** for both.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The layout buttons only show when your project has a camera.
</Note>

## Make your camera look good

Click the **Camera** icon in the right sidebar (or press **Command + 3**).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-camera.png" alt="The Camera panel with looks, brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth" />
</Frame>

Here you can change:

- **Camera shape, size and roundness**: **Rounded**, **Circle** or **Square**, big or small.
- **Camera position**: which corner the bubble sits in.
- **Camera look**: quick color styles like **Natural**, **Studio**, **Golden**, **Cool** or **Mono**. Or adjust **brightness**, **contrast**, **saturation** and **warmth** yourself.
- **Zoom on face** and **Follow face**: keeps your face centered and filling the frame.
- **Remove background**: hides what's behind you and puts a blur or a plain color there instead. This happens on your Mac.
- **Mirror camera**: flips your camera left to right.

## Jump cuts

When you cut a mistake out of a talking-head video, your face can "jump" a little. Cutaway can hide this by zooming your camera in slightly after each cut, so it looks like a deliberate camera change. Find it under **Jump cuts** in the Camera panel.

## Undo

- **Undo**: press **Command + Z**, or the curved arrow in the top bar.
- **Redo** (undo the undo): press **Shift + Command + Z**.

## Saving

Cutaway **saves your work for you** as you go. You don't need to press Save. (If you like, **Command + S** saves right now.)

Your projects are listed on the **Projects** screen. Click one to open it.

## Versions

Cutaway keeps **versions** of your video, like snapshots. For example, before and after the first cut. You can look at an old version and bring it back.

Click the **History** icon (the clock) at the bottom of the right sidebar.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-history.png" alt="The History panel listing versions: AI first cut, and Before: AI first cut" />
</Frame>

- **View**: look at that version. You can't change it while you look. Press **Back to latest** when you're done.
- **Restore**: make that version your current video again. Your current video is saved as a version first, so nothing is lost.
- **Save a version now**: take a snapshot before you try something big.

## Your original recording is safe

Cutaway never changes your original recording. Cuts and effects are saved as instructions, so you can always bring removed parts back.

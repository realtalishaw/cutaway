**Captions** are your words, written on the video as you say them. Lots of people watch videos with the sound off, so captions help everyone follow along.

Cutaway makes captions from your [transcript](/edit/edit-by-transcript), on your Mac.

</Frame>

## Turn captions on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Captions panel">
    Click the **Captions** icon in the right sidebar (or press **Command + 4**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch off Hide transcript">
    When **Hide transcript** is off, your captions show on the video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a style">
    - **Style**: **Pill**, **Outline** or **Bold**.
    - **Position**: **Bottom** or **Top** of the video.
    - **Transcript size**: drag the slider to make the words bigger or smaller.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fix a wrong word

Captions sometimes mishear a word. Press **Edit transcript**, select the word and click **Fix spelling**. Only the caption changes. Your video doesn't.

## Captions file

Every export also makes a **captions file** (called `captions.srt`). You can upload it to YouTube, so viewers can turn captions on and off themselves. This happens even when the captions aren't shown on the video.

The **timeline** is the strip along the bottom of the editor. It shows your whole video from start (left) to end (right).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/clip-selected.png" alt="The timeline with a clip selected. A small bar above the preview shows Split and more options." />
</Frame>

It has three rows:

1. **Text and effects**: text, zooms and callouts you've added.
2. **Video**: your clips. The wavy line is the sound of your voice.
3. **Audio**: music and sound effects.

## Move around

- **Click** anywhere on the timeline to jump there. The purple **playhead** line moves to that spot.
- Press **Space** to play or pause.
- Press **←** or **→** to move one frame. Hold **Shift** to move one second.
- Use the **zoom slider** to the right of the play controls to see more detail on the timeline.

## Cut out a part

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the start">
    Move the playhead to where the part you don't want begins. Press **I**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the end">
    Move the playhead to where it ends. Press **O**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut it">
    Press **X**, or click the **scissors** button next to the play controls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The easiest way to cut out a mistake is by selecting the words in the transcript. See [Edit by transcript](/edit/edit-by-transcript).
</Tip>

Cut parts show as striped areas on the timeline. Nothing is deleted for good. You can always bring it back.

## Split a clip

Splitting chops one clip into two pieces, so you can move or remove one of them.

Move the playhead to where you want to split and press **S** (or **Command + B**). You can also click the small **scissors** on the playhead.

## Trim a clip

Click a clip to select it. Then drag its **left or right edge** to make it shorter. Drag it back out to undo the trim.

## Reorder and remove clips

- **Reorder**: drag a clip left or right.
- **Remove**: select a clip and press **Backspace**. Removed clips can be put back.

## Add more video

Press the **+** at the end of the video row, then pick a video or photo from your Mac.

## Transitions

A **transition** is a smooth change from one clip to the next, instead of a sudden jump. You need two or more clips for this.

Click the small button where two clips meet on the timeline, then choose **cross-dissolve** (one blends into the other), **dip to black** or **dip to white**.

## Undo

Made a mistake? Press **Command + Z** to undo. See [Undo and versions](/edit/undo-and-versions).

The **timeline** is the strip along the bottom of the editor. It shows your whole video from start (left) to end (right).

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/clip-selected.png" alt="The timeline with a clip selected. A small bar above the preview shows Split and more options." />
</Frame>

It has three rows:

1. **Text and effects**: text, zooms and callouts you've added.
2. **Video**: your clips. The wavy line is the sound of your voice.
3. **Audio**: music and sound effects.

## Move around

- **Click** anywhere on the timeline to jump there. The purple **playhead** line moves to that spot.
- Press **Space** to play or pause.
- Press **←** or **→** to move one frame. Hold **Shift** to move one second.
- Use the **zoom slider** to the right of the play controls to see more detail on the timeline.
- Small **flags** on the ruler mark moments you flagged while recording. Click one to jump there, or press **[** and **]** to go to the previous or next flag.

## Cut out a part

<Steps>
  <Step title="Mark the start">
    Move the playhead to where the part you don't want begins. Press **I**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the end">
    Move the playhead to where it ends. Press **O**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut it">
    Press **X**, or click the **scissors** button next to the play controls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The easiest way to cut out a mistake is by selecting the words in the transcript. See [Edit by transcript](/edit/edit-by-transcript).
</Tip>

Cut parts show as striped areas on the timeline. Nothing is deleted for good. You can always bring it back.

## Split a clip

Splitting chops one clip into two pieces, so you can move or remove one of them.

Move the playhead to where you want to split and press **S** (or **Command + B**). You can also click the small **scissors** on the playhead.

## Trim a clip

Click a clip to select it. Then drag its **left or right edge** to make it shorter. Drag it back out to undo the trim.

## Reorder and remove clips

- **Reorder**: drag a clip left or right.
- **Remove**: select a clip and press **Backspace**. Removed clips can be put back.

## The clip panel

Click a clip to open its panel on the right. Here you can:

- give the clip a **Name**,
- press **Earlier** or **Later** to move it one place in the order (or press **⌥←** / **⌥→**),
- press **Split at playhead**,
- pick a **View**: what the clip shows, such as **Screen only** or **Camera only**. **Automatic** lets Cutaway choose.

When you remove a clip, it isn't gone. It moves to **Off the timeline** in the panel. Press **Put back at the end** (or **Put back**) to bring it back.

## Add more video or photos

Press the **+** at the end of the video row, then choose **Video or photo from my files…** and pick files from your Mac.

A **photo** becomes a clip of its own. It shows for 5 seconds at first. To change how long it shows:

- click the photo clip, press **Length** in the bar above the preview, and drag **Shown for**, or
- drag the right edge of the photo clip on the timeline.

<Tip>
  Want a picture or video to show **on top of** your footage while your voice keeps playing? That's B-roll. See [B-roll](/edit/b-roll).
</Tip>

## Select a stretch of the timeline

Drag across the video row to select a stretch of time. You can also press **I** at the start and **O** at the end. Double-click the footage to select the part between two cuts.

The panel on the right then shows what you can do with that stretch:

- **Cut**: remove it. **Restore** brings back any parts already cut inside it.
- **Keep as is** (or press **L**): AI changes will leave this part alone.
- **Speed**: play this part at **0.5×**, **1×**, **1.5×**, **2×** or **4×**. Your captions follow the new speed.
- **Show here**: pick what shows in this part, such as **Screen only**, **Screen + camera**, **Camera** or **Split**.
- **Add**: put a **Zoom**, **Text**, **Callout**, **Section card**, **Lower third** or **Mask** over this part.
- **Ask for a change here**: describe a change in your own words, like "Less movement in this section", and press **Preview the change**.

When you preview a change, a bar appears above the preview. Switch between **Before** and **After** to compare. Press **Accept** to keep the change, or **Discard** to throw it away. Only the part you selected changes.

## Work with several blocks at once

Hold **Shift** or **Command** and click blocks on the timeline to select more than one. Click a block again the same way to take it out.

With blocks selected you can:

- drag one to move them all,
- press **⌥←** or **⌥→** to nudge them one frame (add **Shift** for one second),
- **Copy** them (**Command + C**) and **Paste at the playhead** (**Command + V**),
- **Duplicate** them (**Command + D**),
- **Remove all** of them (or press **Backspace**).

You'll find **Copy**, **Paste at the playhead** and **Duplicate** in the **…** menu above the preview, or by right-clicking a block.

## Snapping

When you drag a block, its edges snap to useful spots: the playhead, clip changes, flags and the edges of other blocks. A thin line shows where it snapped.

Hold **Option** while you drag to turn snapping off.

## Transitions

A **transition** is a smooth change from one clip to the next, instead of a sudden jump. You need two or more clips for this.

Click the small button where two clips meet on the timeline, then choose **Cross-dissolve** (one blends into the other), **Dip to black** or **Dip to white**. **Hard cut** means no transition.

- **Length**: drag to make the transition shorter or longer.
- **Use on every clip change**: in the clip panel, copies this transition to every place where two clips meet.
- **Remove all**: in the clip panel, turns every transition back into a hard cut.

A transition never changes how long your video is.

## Simple and Advanced timeline

Next to the play controls is an **Advanced** switch. The simple timeline shows three rows: text and effects, video, and audio.

Turn **Advanced** on to see every layer on its own row, such as views, masks, speed changes and sound effects. A button above the preview then lets you pick **1 visible timeline** or **3 visible timelines** (or press **Command + T**).

## Undo

Made a mistake? Press **Command + Z** to undo. See [Undo and versions](/edit/undo-and-versions).

## Add text

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the moment">
    Click the timeline where the text should appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add text">
    Press **T**, or click the **T** at the bottom of the tools on the left of the preview. "Your text" appears on the video, and a text block appears on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your words">
    In the panel on the right, type in **Words**. Add a **Second line** if you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Drag the text on the preview to move it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Make it look nice

In the text panel you can change the **font**, **size**, **weight** (how thick the letters are), **color**, and add a **panel** (a box behind the words), an **outline** or a **shadow**.

You can also make text appear with a **fade**, **slide**, **pop** or **typewriter** animation.

## Use a ready-made title

Click the **T** icon in the right sidebar to see ready-made designs. Click one to add it at the playhead.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screens/tab-text.png" alt="The Text and titles panel with title designs like Big title, Name bar, Chapter card and End card" />
</Frame>

- **Titles**: big words for the start of your video, like **Big title** or **Title and subtitle**.
- **Lower thirds**: a name label near the bottom, like **Name bar**. Great when you introduce yourself.
- **Cards**: a full screen card, like a **Chapter card** or a **Thanks for watching** end card.

## Change how long it shows

Drag the ends of the text block on the timeline. To remove it, click it and press **Backspace**.

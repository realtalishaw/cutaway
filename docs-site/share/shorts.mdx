**Shorts** are short, upright videos for phones, like YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Instagram Reels. Cutaway can find the best 30 to 60 seconds of your video and turn it into one.

Your main video is never changed.

## Make a short

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shorts">
    Click **Shorts** in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Find shorts">
    Cutaway suggests the strongest moments. Each suggestion appears on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep the ones you like">
    Press **Keep** on a suggestion. Press **Dismiss** to drop it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust it (optional)">
    On the right you can change:
    - **Range**: where the short starts and ends.
    - **Frame**: **Vertical 9:16** or **Square 1:1**. Choose **Face** to follow your face, **Stack** to put your screen above your face, or **Screen follow** to follow your mouse.
    - **Captions**: pick a caption style. The word you're saying lights up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export it">
    Press **Export this short**. Each short is saved as its own video file. Made several? **Export all** saves them all at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Want to pick the moment yourself? Press **By hand** instead of **Find shorts**.
</Tip>

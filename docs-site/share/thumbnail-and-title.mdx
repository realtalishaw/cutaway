A **thumbnail** is the small picture people see before they click your video. A good thumbnail and title help people choose your video.

Cutaway makes three ideas for you, from real moments in your video and the words you said.

  <img src="/images/annotated/package-tour.png" alt="The Package page. The three ideas on the left are marked 1, Choose this pair 2, the big thumbnail 3, and the AI thumbnail panel 4" />
</Frame>

1. **Concepts**: your three thumbnail and title ideas.
2. **Choose this pair**: picks the one you'll export.
3. **Thumbnail**: the one you're working on, big.
4. **AI thumbnail**: make it again, with your photo, your logo and your changes.

## Make a thumbnail and title

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Package">
    Click **Package** in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Create three concepts">
    Cutaway makes three thumbnail and title ideas, each built on a thumbnail format that is working on YouTube right now (like **Glowing Icon**, **Cardboard Sign** or **Wrong vs Right**). If **Codex** or a **fal.ai** key is set up, it then makes each thumbnail as one finished AI picture: you, the props and the words (it asks your permission the first time).

    If neither is set up, Cutaway asks first, because thumbnails look much better with a picture. Press **Add a fal.ai key** (**1** below) to set one up, or **Continue without pictures** (**2**) to get thumbnails made from frames of your video and text.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/art-gate.png" alt="The Connect an image generator for better thumbnails window, with Add a fal.ai key marked 1 and Continue without pictures marked 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one">
    Press **Choose this pair** under the one you like best. The project is renamed to that title, and its card in your projects list shows that thumbnail. (Until you choose one, the card shows a frame from your video.)
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/screens/library.png" alt="The Projects screen. The project card shows the chosen AI thumbnail" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change anything">
    - Click the words on the thumbnail to change them.
    - Pick a different **Layout**: **Headline**, **Banner** or **Badge**.
    - Change the **Title** in the box under the thumbnail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add layers

A thumbnail is made of **Layers**, stacked on top of each other. You'll find them on the right. Use the small buttons next to **Layers** to add:

- **Add text**: new words you can type.
- **Add a shape**: a block, pill or circle of colour.
- **Add the frame at the playhead**: a real picture from your video, at the spot where the playhead is.
- **Add an image file**: a picture from your Mac, like a logo.

Click a layer to change it. Drag it on the thumbnail to move it, or use the arrow keys to nudge it. Use **Bring forward** and **Send back** to change the order. **Delete layer** removes it.

## AI thumbnails

With **Codex** or a **fal.ai** key set up, each thumbnail is designed and made as one finished picture, the way top YouTube thumbnails look: you with a strong expression, one or two props that tell the story, and a few huge words. Under **AI thumbnail** on the right, you can see which format it uses and why.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/art-variations.png" alt="A finished AI thumbnail marked 1. Under AI thumbnail, two versions are marked 2, with Use and In use buttons, and Back to the simple thumbnail is marked 3" />
</Frame>

- **Make another:** a new version. Earlier versions stay underneath (**2** above); press **Use** to swap one back in.
- **Ask for a change:** type it in the box first, like "make me look surprised" or "use a red background", then press **Make another**.
- **Back to the simple thumbnail** (**3**): go back to the layout made from a frame of your video. Your AI thumbnails stay in the list.

It uses whichever of these is set up:

- **Codex**, signed in with your ChatGPT account. Used first if it's ready. About a minute per thumbnail.
- **fal.ai**, with your own API key in **Settings → AI generation**. About 20 seconds per thumbnail, and each one spends a little of your fal.ai credit.

The first time in each project, Cutaway asks before it sends anything:

### Use your own photo

By default, Cutaway builds thumbnails on a frame of you from your video's camera. A clear, well-lit photo, like a studio headshot, looks much better:

1. Under **AI thumbnail**, press **Use a photo…** (**1** above) and choose the photo.
2. Make the thumbnail again.

Your photo is saved once and used for every project. Press **Use the video** to go back to frames from the video.

### Add your logo

Without a picture of your logo, the AI draws a made-up icon when a thumbnail shows your product. Save your real logo and it's copied into the picture instead:

1. Under **AI thumbnail**, press **Add your logo…** (**2** above) and choose a picture of it. A PNG with a transparent or plain background works best.
2. Check the name next to it. Cutaway guesses it from the file name (`acme-logo-final.png` becomes "Acme"). Change it to what people call your product.
3. Press **New concepts** so the new thumbnail ideas know about it, then make the thumbnails.

You can save up to three logos, and they're used in every project. A logo only goes into a thumbnail whose idea shows it, so a thumbnail about something else doesn't get one stuck on.

<Note>
  To make a thumbnail, its design, the title words, your photo (or a frame of you) and any logo it shows are sent to OpenAI (Codex) or fal.ai. Cutaway asks before the first one for each project.
</Note>

## Pick a different title

Under the **Title** box, **Alternatives** shows the titles from your other ideas. Press **Use here** to swap one in. The alternatives are also saved in your YouTube package.

## Check before you publish

If something might hurt your title or thumbnail, a box called **Worth a look before you publish** appears. For example, it warns you when:

- the title is too long for a phone screen, or over YouTube's 100-character limit
- the title is all capitals
- the thumbnail has more than five words
- the thumbnail repeats the title

These are only tips. Cutaway never changes your words for you.

## See how it looks on YouTube

Under **At browsing size**, Cutaway shows your thumbnail as small as it'll look in a list of videos. Make sure the words are still easy to read.

## Write a description and chapters

Further down the page:

- **Description**: what your video is about, in your own words. It's saved as **description.txt**. If you leave it empty, it's left out.
- **Chapters**: the parts of your video, shown on YouTube's progress bar. Press **Add first chapter**, then **Add at playhead** for each new part. YouTube needs at least three chapters, each ten seconds or longer, starting at 00:00. When they're right, you'll see **Valid for YouTube**.

<Tip>
  Chapter times follow your edit. If you cut something later, the times update on their own.
</Tip>

## Tips for beginners

- **Short words win.** Three or four big words are easier to read than a sentence.
- **Show a face.** Thumbnails with a face often get more clicks.
- **Say what people get.** For example, "Share a list in 1 minute".

<Note>
  The thumbnail and title are saved when you export a **YouTube package**. See [Export](/share/export).
</Note>

## What you'll do

Instead of one video file, you'll export a **YouTube package**: a folder you can upload from, with every piece ready.

## Before you start

- Pick a thumbnail and title in **Package** and press **Choose this pair**. See [Make a click-worthy AI thumbnail](/tutorials/ai-thumbnail).
- Optional: add a **Description** and **Chapters**, lower down on the **Package** page.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Export">
    It's the purple button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose YouTube package">
    Under **Export to**, press **YouTube package** (**1** below). The list under it shows every file the folder will have.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/export-youtube.png" alt="The Export page with YouTube package marked 1 and the Export YouTube package button marked 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Preflight">
    On the right, **Preflight** checks everything first. A green tick means ready. If something needs a look, it says what to do, with a button that takes you there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Export YouTube package">
    It's at the bottom (**2**). Keep Cutaway open until it's done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in the folder

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **video.mp4** | Your finished video |
| **thumbnail.jpg** | The thumbnail you chose, ready to upload |
| **thumbnail-master.jpg** | A big 4K copy of the thumbnail |
| **title.txt** | Your title. Other title ideas are in **title-options.txt** |
| **description.txt** | Your description, if you wrote one |
| **chapters.txt** | Your chapter times, to paste into YouTube's description |
| **captions.srt** | Captions timed to your final cut. Upload it in YouTube Studio under **Subtitles** |
| **credits.txt** | Where your B-roll and music came from |
| **package.json** | A record of the version, settings and checks, for other apps |

Files with nothing to say are left out. For example, with no chapters there's no **chapters.txt**. When it's done, press **Reveal in Finder** to open the folder.

<Note>
  **YouTube package** needs **MP4** in the **Wide 16:9** shape. If it's greyed out, see [Export](/share/export).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Not ready to choose a thumbnail yet? You can still export a draft: switch on **I understand this is a draft**. The folder name ends in **-draft**.
</Tip>

## What you'll do

Record a short video of your screen, let Cutaway remove the pauses and "um"s, and save it as a video file you can share.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press New recording">
    It's the purple button on the **Projects** screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Display">
    In the bar at the bottom, **Display** records your whole screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a layout">
    **Screen + bubble** shows your screen with your face in a small bubble. Pick **Screen only** if you don't want your face in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your camera and microphone">
    Click **No camera** and **No microphone** in the bar and pick yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Start recording">
    After the countdown, show what you want to show and talk as you go. Press **stop** on the small floating bar when you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press First cut, then Create first cut">
    When you stop, a **Recording saved** box appears. Press **First cut**, then press **Create first cut**. Cutaway removes long pauses and filler words, and puts your video together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Play to watch it">
    Or press the **Space** bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Export, then Export to file">
    Your video is saved as an **MP4** file in **Movies → Cutaway Exports**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Don't worry about making mistakes while you record. Just pause, and say it again. You can cut out the bad take afterwards. See [Cut out mistakes](/tutorials/cut-out-mistakes).
</Tip>

## What you'll do

You'll turn on captions so people can follow along with the sound off, then add some background music.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Captions">
    Click the **Captions** icon in the right sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch off Hide transcript">
    Now your words show on the video as you say them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a style">
    Try **Pill**, **Outline** or **Bold**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add music">
    Press the **+** at the end of the audio row (the bottom row of the timeline). Choose **Music or sound from my files…** and pick a song from your Mac.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it">
    The music gets quieter by itself while you talk, so your voice is easy to hear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only use music you're allowed to use. Look for "royalty-free" music.
</Warning>

Learn more in [Captions](/edit/captions) and [Voice, music and sound](/edit/music-and-sound).

## You're ready

That's the basics. When you're happy with your video, [export it](/share/export).

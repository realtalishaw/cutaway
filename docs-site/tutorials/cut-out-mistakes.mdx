## What you'll do

In the video, the speaker says "Let's call it" twice. You'll remove the extra one by selecting the words, like editing a document.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Transcript">
    Click the **Transcript** icon (the lines) in the right sidebar. You'll see everything you said, written out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the mistake">
    Read through the text. Look for words said twice, or things you didn't mean to say.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag across the words you don't want">
    Click the first word and drag to the last one. They turn highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Cut">
    The words are crossed out, and that part of the video is gone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it">
    Click just before the cut and press **Play** to check it sounds right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Cut too much? Select the crossed-out words and press **Restore**. Nothing is ever deleted for good.
</Tip>

Learn more in [Edit by transcript](/edit/edit-by-transcript).

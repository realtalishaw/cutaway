## What you'll do

You'll let Cutaway make three finished thumbnails with AI, then make one better: built on a clear photo of you, with your real logo in it, and the change you ask for.

<Frame caption="Before and after: the first try, then the one with a photo, the logo and a change.">
  <img src="/images/annotated/art-variations.png" alt="The Package page. The big thumbnail shows the creator holding a phone with the Listful logo. Under AI thumbnail, two versions sit side by side, with Use and In use buttons." />
</Frame>

## Before you start

You need one thing that can make pictures:

- **Codex**, signed in with your ChatGPT account, or
- a **fal.ai** API key, added in **Settings → AI generation**. Each thumbnail spends a little of your fal.ai credit (about 15 cents).

Don't have either? Cutaway asks when you start, and you can still get simple thumbnails made from frames of your video. See [Thumbnail and title](/share/thumbnail-and-title).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Package and press Create three concepts">
    Click **Package** in the top bar, then press **Create three concepts**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/package.png" alt="The empty Package page with the Create three concepts button marked 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow it">
    The first time, Cutaway tells you what it will send and that it uses your own fal.ai credit. Press **Allow fal.ai for this project** (or the Codex button, if you use Codex).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/art-consent.png" alt="The window that asks before making thumbnail pictures with fal.ai, with Allow fal.ai for this project marked 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait about a minute">
    Cutaway makes the three thumbnails one after another. You can look around while it works.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/art-making.png" alt="A progress box on the left that says Making thumbnails with fal.ai: 1 of 3" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use a photo of yourself">
    On the right, under **AI thumbnail**, press **Use a photo…** and pick a clear, well-lit photo of yourself (marked **1** below). A studio headshot works great. Cutaway keeps it for every project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your logo">
    Press **Add your logo…** and pick your logo (**2**). A PNG with a transparent or plain background works best. Check the name next to it: it's how the AI knows when to show it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a change, then press Make another">
    Type what you want in the box (**3**), for example "Show the Listful logo on a phone in my hand". Then press **Make another** (**4**).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/art-panel.png" alt="The AI thumbnail panel with four numbered parts: the photo, the logo, the change box, and the Make another button" />
    </Frame>

    The new thumbnail goes straight in. Earlier ones stay underneath: press **Use** to swap one back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Choose this pair">
    Happy? On the left, press **Choose this pair** under it. The project is renamed to that title, and its card in your projects list shows the thumbnail.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/package-chosen.png" alt="The chosen thumbnail. The button on the left now says Chosen pair, and the thumbnail says Chosen for export" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Small screens matter. Scroll down to **At browsing size** to see your thumbnail as small as it will look on YouTube. If you can't read the words, ask for fewer, bigger words.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your photo, the thumbnail's design, its words and any logo it shows are sent to fal.ai (or OpenAI, for Codex). Your video and sound never leave your Mac.
</Note>

Learn more in [Thumbnail and title](/share/thumbnail-and-title).

## What you'll do

You'll connect Cutaway to **Claude Code** or **Codex** (one click), then ask for a finished video in plain words. The AI works inside the Cutaway window while you watch, and every change it makes stays editable.

## Before you start

You need **Claude Code** or **Codex** installed on your Mac and signed in. See [Set up the AI helpers](/ai/set-up-ai).

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Cutaway">
    In Cutaway, press **Command + ,** and go to **AI tools**. Press **Connect** next to **Claude Code** or **Codex**. When it says **Connected**, you're done.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/screens/settings-ai-tools.png" alt="Settings, AI tools tab, with Let AI tools control Cutaway switched on and Connect buttons for Claude Code and Codex" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new chat">
    Open a **new** session in Claude Code or Codex, so it can see Cutaway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the whole video">
    Say what you want, in one message or a few. Name your recording's file if it isn't in Cutaway yet. For example:

    > Make a video from `~/Movies/listful-demo.mov` in Cutaway. Make a first cut, remove the part where I repeat myself, zoom in when I add the items, add a title at the start that says "Share a list in 1 minute", turn on bold captions, and add quiet music from `~/Music/bed.mp3`. Show me when you're done.

    Cutaway comes to the front so you can watch each change land.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check its work">
    Every change the AI makes is saved in **History** as "AI tool: …". Click one to look at that version, or **Restore** an earlier one.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/screens/tab-history.png" alt="The History panel listing earlier versions of the video" />
    </Frame>

    Open **AI review** (the sparkle icon) to see what was cut and put anything back. If the AI wasn't sure about cutting something you said, it's waiting there as a suggestion for you to approve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish by hand, or ask for more">
    Keep editing yourself, or ask for the next change, like "make the intro shorter" or "export it as an MP4".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good things to ask for

- "Make a first cut of `~/Movies/demo.mov` and show me."
- "Remove the part where I say 'let me try that again'."
- "Zoom in when I open the settings page."
- "Add a subscribe animation near the end."
- "Turn on bold captions."
- "Export it as an MP4."

<Tip>
  Ask it to **show you** after each change. The AI can look at a picture of the video to check its own work.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your video and sound files stay on your Mac. The AI tool sees text, like your transcript and a description of the edit, plus the pictures it asks for to check its work. Turn it off any time with **Let AI tools control Cutaway** in **Settings → AI tools**.
</Note>

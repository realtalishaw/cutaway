## What you'll do

You'll record while reading a script, without looking away from the camera. The teleprompter floats in its own window, and it's always left out of the recording.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter.png" alt="The teleprompter window showing a script in big text, with its buttons numbered 1 to 6" />
</Frame>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the teleprompter">
    Press **New recording**. Under the purple **Start recording** button, press **Teleprompter** (**2** below).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/record-start.png" alt="Start recording marked 1, with the Teleprompter button under it marked 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your script">
    The first time, it opens on the script editor (**1**). Type or paste what you want to say, with an empty line between paragraphs. The bar at the bottom (**2**) shows how many words you have and about how long they take to read.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter-edit.png" alt="The script editor with the script box marked 1, the word count marked 2, and the Done button marked 3" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press ✓ (Done)">
    It's the tick at the top (**3**). Now your script shows in big text, ready to read.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it under your camera">
    Press the **layout** button and choose **Under the camera**, so your eyes stay close to the lens while you read. You can also drag the window anywhere, and drag an edge to resize it.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/teleprompter-places.png" alt="The list of quick spots for the teleprompter, marked 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Start recording and read">
    The script starts scrolling when the recording starts, pauses when you pause, and stops when you stop. Read at your own pace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## While you read

Using the numbers in the picture at the top:

1. **Play** starts or pauses scrolling (or press **Space**).
2. The time left at this pace.
3. The **pencil** goes back to editing your script.
4. The **layout** button moves it to a quick spot.
5. The **gear** has the pace, text size, background and **Mirror the text** (for teleprompter glass).
6. The pace, in words per minute. Press **+** or **−** (or the **↑** and **↓** keys) to go faster or slower.

<Tip>
  Lost your place? Press **←** to go back a line. Changing the text size doesn't change your reading speed.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Your script stays on your Mac. The teleprompter is never part of your video, even when you record your whole screen.
</Note>

Learn more in [Teleprompter](/record/teleprompter).

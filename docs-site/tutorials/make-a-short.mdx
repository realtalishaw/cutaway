## What you'll do

You'll make an upright video for YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Instagram Reels from your main video. Your main video doesn't change.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shorts and press Find shorts">
    Click **Shorts** in the top bar, then press **Find shorts** (**1** below). Cutaway suggests the strongest moments. Want to pick the moment yourself? Press **By hand** (**2**).
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/shorts.png" alt="The Shorts page with Find shorts marked 1 and By hand marked 2" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Keep">
    Suggestions appear on the left. Press **Keep** on the one you like. You can change a short once you've kept it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it yours">
    On the right:
    - **Title** (**3** below): a name for the short.
    - **Range** (**4**): the minus and plus buttons move the start or end by one sentence, so it never cuts you off mid-word.
    - **Frame** (**5**): **Face** follows your face, **Stack** puts your screen above your face, **Screen follow** follows your mouse, **As edited** keeps the views from your main video.
    - **Captions** (**6**): pick a style. The word you're saying lights up.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/shorts-found.png" alt="A kept short. Its card is marked 1, the upright preview 2, the title 3, the range buttons 4, the frame choices 5 and the caption styles 6" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it">
    Press play under the preview to check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Export this short">
    It's at the bottom of the panel on the right. Each short is saved as its own video file. Made several? **Export all** saves them all at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Find shorts** uses your AI helper if one is set up. Press **Rules only** to keep everything on your Mac.
</Tip>

Learn more in [Shorts](/share/shorts).

## What you'll do

Your recording shows the browser's tabs and address bar. They're distracting, and they can show things you'd rather keep private, like other tabs. You'll crop them away so only the web page is in your video.

<Frame caption="Before: tabs and address bar. After: only the page.">
  <img src="/images/screens/browser-before.png" alt="The editor showing a recording of a browser, with its tabs and address bar at the top" />
</Frame>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Crop">
    It's above the preview, next to the video shape.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Hide browser bar">
    Under **Recorded a browser?**, press **Hide browser bar** (**1** below). Cutaway looks at frames across your whole recording and finds the tabs, the address bar and any side bars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the frame">
    The crop frame (**2**) now starts just below the address bar. If a sliver of the bar still shows, drag the top edge down a little.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/crop-browser-bar.png" alt="The Crop the recording window. Hide browser bar is marked 1, the crop frame below the address bar is marked 2, and Apply crop is marked 3" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Apply crop">
    It's at the bottom right (**3**). The crop applies to your whole video.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/annotated/browser-after.png" alt="The editor after the crop. The preview shows only the web page, without the browser's tabs or address bar" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Next time, record with **Tab** instead of **Display** or **Window**. It leaves the tab strip and toolbar out from the start. See [Start a recording](/record/start-a-recording#tab-keep-the-tab-in-front).
</Tip>

<Note>
  Changed your mind? Press **Crop** again and press **Reset**, or press **Command + Z**.
</Note>

## What you'll do

You'll add a zoom so viewers can clearly see the shopping list being filled in.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the timeline where the zoom should start">
    The purple playhead line moves there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the magnifying glass">
    It's at the top of the tools on the left of the preview. A purple zoom block appears on the timeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to zoom into">
    Press **Click the preview to set the target**, then click the spot on the preview you want to zoom into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Zoom in more or less">
    Drag the **Magnification** slider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it">
    Click a moment before the zoom and press **Play**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Want the zoom to last longer? Drag the right edge of the zoom block on the timeline.
</Tip>

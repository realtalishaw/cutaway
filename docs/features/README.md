# Cutaway features

A quick list of what Cutaway does, with a line or two on each. For setup and
the basic workflow, see the [main README](../../README.md).

## Recording

- **Screen, window, area or browser tab.** Record a whole display, one window,
  a dragged-out area, or a single browser tab (just the page, without the tab
  strip and toolbar).
- **iPhone and iPad screens.** Record a connected device's screen.
- **Two cameras.** Record your Mac's camera and a second angle (for example an
  iPhone through Continuity Camera), each as its own track.
- **Microphone and system audio.** Your voice and the Mac's sound, each on its
  own track. System audio can be limited to chosen apps.
- **Layouts.** Screen with a camera bubble, side by side, camera with a screen
  inset, screen only, or camera only. Chosen before recording, changeable
  after.
- **Countdown** over the display being recorded, with the target outlined.
- **Floating controls** to pause, flag a moment, or stop. Never recorded.
- **Camera bubble** so you can see yourself while recording. Never recorded.
- **Teleprompter.** Your script in big text, scrolling at a words-per-minute
  pace past a reading line. Put it anywhere (under the camera, at either side,
  over your camera bubble); it starts, pauses and stops with the recording.
  Never recorded.
- **Record Cutaway too.** A demo mode that includes Cutaway's own window, so
  you can record how-to videos of Cutaway itself, while still leaving the
  controls, teleprompter and camera bubble out.
- **Flags.** Mark moments while recording; they show up on the timeline.
- **Clean recordings.** Hide desktop icons and notification banners from the
  recording without changing anything on your Mac.
- **Crash-safe.** Footage is saved every two seconds; an interrupted take is
  recovered up to that point.

## First cut (AI)

- **Assemble a first cut** from your brief: pace, style, what to keep.
- **Removes** pauses, filler words ("um", "uh") and retakes.
- **Picks camera layouts** and adds **zooms** where you click.
- **Places your B-roll** and images.
- **Review** lists every decision with its reason; anything uncertain is
  flagged for your call. Accept or dismiss each one.
- Works **without AI** too: built-in rules make a first cut on your Mac.

## Editing

- **Simple timeline.** Rows for video, text and effects, and audio, with clip
  blocks you can see and select. **Advanced** shows every track.
- **Cut and arrange.** Split at the playhead (⌘B), trim by dragging edges,
  reorder by dragging, delete, and put removed clips back. Clips snap to the
  playhead and to each other.
- **Click to select.** Click the footage in the preview to select its clip.
- **Multi-select, copy and paste, nudge.** Shift-click or drag to select
  several; ⌘C / ⌘V; arrow keys move selected items.
- **Edit by transcript.** Delete words in the transcript to cut them from the
  video.
- **Photos as clips.** Put still images on the video row and set how long each
  shows.
- **Zooms and cursor.** Zoom in on any part of the screen; the cursor can be
  smoothed, enlarged or hidden.
- **Background and shape.** Gradient, colour or image background, rounded
  corners, shadow and padding around the recording.
- **Text and titles.** A free text tool and title templates, with fonts,
  colours, outlines and animations.
- **Callouts and masks.** Point things out, or blur and hide private
  information. Cutaway can also find likely sensitive details (emails, keys) for
  you.
- **Transitions.** Cross-dissolve and dips to black or white between clips.
- **Speed.** Speed up slow stretches.
- **Punch-ins.** Alternate framing at jump cuts in talking-head footage.
- **Undo and versions.** ⌘Z for everything; named versions you can view and
  restore from History.

## Audio

- **Music.** Add music to the timeline, split it, set fades, draw volume
  points, loop it, and lower it automatically under speech. Waveforms show on
  the clips.
- **Record a voiceover** straight onto the audio row.
- **Sound effects.** Built-in effects, or your own files, plus one-step options
  to add a sound on every cut or text entry.
- **Voice cleanup** for the main microphone.

## Animations

- **Templates.** Subscribe button, name tag, title card, counter, checklist,
  and boxed annotations (arrow, circle, highlighter) that you place over
  anything on screen by dragging a box.
- **Describe your own.** Type what you want ("a burst of gold confetti") and
  your AI writes it; Cutaway tests it in its renderer and has it fixed if
  needed. You see each step as it happens.
- **Edit words and colours** of any animation in the side panel.
- **Save as template** to reuse your own animations in any project.
- **Ideas from what you say.** Review spots moments that suit an animation
  ("subscribe", "my name is…", "10,000 users", "step two", "click here") and
  offers to add them, timed to the words.
- Animations render the same in the preview and the export, with their sounds.

## Ask AI

- **Chat about your edit** (⌘9): ask questions about the video or describe a
  change. Proposed changes come as cards you apply one at a time.
- **Animations from chat.** "Add a subscribe button when I say subscribe" adds
  the template at the right moment; anything custom opens the animation sheet
  to be written.
- Every applied change becomes a **version** you can view or restore.

## AI generation (your own keys)

With your own **ElevenLabs** or **fal.ai** key:

- **Voiceovers** from text
- **Music** and **sound effects** from a description
- **Images** and short **video clips** for B-roll

## Captions

- **Automatic captions** from speech, transcribed on your Mac.
- Choose a **caption style**, burn them into the video, or export a caption
  file.

## Package and export

- **Titles and thumbnails.** Three title and thumbnail concepts from your
  video; pick one, change anything, preview at YouTube size.
- **Shorts.** Finds 30–60 second moments, reframes them vertically, adds
  captions, and exports each one.
- **One export folder** with the video, thumbnail, title, description,
  captions and chapters.
- **Quick share** right after recording, for a fast export without editing.

## Privacy

- Everything runs on your Mac: transcription, editing, preview and export.
- AI features use your own Claude Code or Codex sign-in, and only after you
  allow it. They receive the transcript, edit details and a few still frames,
  never your video or audio files.
- Generation keys are stored encrypted and sent only to that service.
- No account, no analytics, no server.
